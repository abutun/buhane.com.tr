Creative context

From Cosmic Meta to Mintropolis

The Cosmic Meta NFT universe provides a creative point of reference, including Mintropolis Genesis. Mintropolis is a distinct product with its own rules, roadmap and public record.

Passport

Participation, not a financial asset

Passport is a free wallet-linked discovery profile. It has no transfer value and does not guarantee mint eligibility. Collection availability and eligibility are determined by the official contract and mint page.

Built in public

A roadmap with visible decision gates

The current focus is Genesis and Passport. Further participation, resources and governance experiments are conditional roadmap work, not features promised today. Follow the public roadmap for the current direction.

Common questions

Is Mintropolis available?

Yes. The public web experience is available in beta, and the scope can evolve as the product is tested.

Is Passport free?

Yes. Passport is a free discovery profile linked to a wallet, with stamps recording participation.

Does a Passport stamp guarantee an NFT mint?

No. A stamp is not a mint allocation or financial entitlement. Review each collection's official source and current conditions.
