Astral Post

For people who want a ritualized way to release a thought and receive a symbolic prompt for reflection.

Some thoughts benefit from expression without needing a conventional conversation. Astral Post provides a message ritual and a symbolic response path.

What does it do?

  • Create a personal message as part of a guided ritual.
  • Receive symbolic reflections intended as prompts, not guaranteed answers.
  • Read or participate in an anonymous public feed.

How it works

  1. 1

    Write the thought or message you want to release.

  2. 2

    Send it through the product's ritual experience.

  3. 3

    Treat the returned reflection as a prompt and decide what it means to you.

Data and safety context

Use it deliberately

Messages may be personal. Avoid including identifying or sensitive information in any public or shared surface, and read the current privacy controls before participating.

Important boundary

What is it not?

Astral Post does not provide medical, legal, or financial advice and cannot guarantee an outcome or interpretation.

Buhane's builder perspective

Why this product?

Buhane built Astral Post as a designed pause: expression comes first, then a symbolic perspective that the user remains free to accept, reinterpret, or leave behind.

Common questions

Is a symbolic reflection professional advice?

No. It is a reflective prompt and is not medical, legal, or financial advice.

Is every message public?

The product includes an anonymous public feed, but review the current flow and privacy choices before sharing.

What should I put in a message?

Use words that help you express the moment while leaving out details you would not want exposed.
