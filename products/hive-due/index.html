Community finance management

One shared workflow for the records a community relies on

Community finance management is the practical work of keeping shared costs, dues, resident information and the communications that explain them connected. It gives administrators and residents a common reference point without treating a community's finances as a pile of unrelated files.

  • Set up units, residents, dues and shared-expense categories in the community context.
  • Use area-based (m²) calculations where appropriate and bring existing records in with bulk import.
  • Record obligations and payments so managers can review what is due while residents can see their own financial status.
  • Keep announcements, documents and reports alongside the operational context they explain.

For managers

Make the next action easier to see

Administrators can review residents, units, dues, recorded payments, advance balances, documents and reports from the same product context. That makes it easier to explain what is outstanding and to prepare the information a community needs.

For residents

Make personal status easier to understand

Residents can use their own view to follow their balances, recorded payments, announcements and the documents available to them. Clearer visibility supports better conversations; it does not replace a community's own policies or financial advice.

A practical community-finance workflow

  1. 1

    Choose the community and organize its units, residents and financial categories.

  2. 2

    Create dues or shared expenses with the relevant due dates, using area-based calculations where the community needs them.

  3. 3

    Record or import the current financial records, including payments and advance balances where applicable.

  4. 4

    Share announcements and documents, then use the available reports to review the current picture.

It means organizing a community's dues, shared expenses, residents, units, financial records and related communications in a connected workflow.

Who is this product for?

It is designed for residential communities and the managers and residents who need a clearer shared view of community financial records.
