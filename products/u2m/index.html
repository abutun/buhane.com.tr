U2M URL Shortener

For people and developers who need a shorter shareable link and a documented way to integrate that job.

Long destination URLs are awkward to share and difficult to recognize in compact spaces. U2M creates a shorter route and keeps measurable link context.

What does it do?

  • Create a shortened URL for a destination link.
  • Use public API documentation when integrating shortening into software.
  • Review link statistics associated with the service.

How it works

  1. 1

    Provide the destination URL you are authorized to shorten.

  2. 2

    Create and copy the resulting short link.

  3. 3

    Use the public documentation or statistics only for the workflow you need.

Data and safety context

Use it deliberately

A destination URL may reveal private paths, tokens, or user information. Remove sensitive parameters and never submit a link you are not authorized to process.

Important boundary

What is it not?

U2M does not claim a redirect-volume total or uptime guarantee on this page. A short link also does not make an unsafe destination trustworthy.

Buhane's builder perspective

Why this product?

Buhane built U2M around a small infrastructure job with two audiences: a person who needs a shareable link and a developer who needs a documented interface.

Common questions

Does U2M provide an API?

Public API documentation is part of the reviewed product capability set.

What information should not be shortened?

Do not submit destinations containing secrets, private tokens, personal records, or URLs you have no right to process.

Do link statistics prove a visitor converted?

No. Link statistics describe link activity; business outcomes require their own measurement context.
