LeaseLore

Get to know an address before committing to a lease.

LeaseLore puts resident experiences next to the home you are considering. Search an address, read structured feedback, check score confidence and keep a shortlist of possible homes.

Explore LeaseLoreGet it on Google Play

A product by Buhane Information Technologies. Meet Buhane.

What does it do?

  • Find a specific home through address and map-based discovery.
  • Compare resident feedback on noise, maintenance, safety, value and commute, with score confidence kept visible.
  • Save homes for later and contribute structured reviews that go through moderation.

How it works

  1. 1

    Search the address or explore homes on the map.

  2. 2

    Read the available reviews and consider the score's freshness and confidence.

  3. 3

    Save a shortlist or share your own experience as a resident.

Review context

Read the evidence behind a score

Review availability depends on community contributions. A score summarizes resident feedback, not a guarantee of a home's condition or suitability. Check the amount and recency of the available information.

Product focus

The home, not just the neighbourhood

LeaseLore keeps discovery tied to the address. Resident notes, saved homes and visible confidence help you compare specific options while keeping the source of each signal in view.

Common questions

Is LeaseLore available for iPhone?

Not on the App Store yet. The official site accepts iOS early-access requests; Android is available on Google Play.

Will every address have reviews?

No. Available reviews depend on resident contributions. A missing or limited record should not be treated as proof of quality.

How are reviews and support handled?

Submissions are moderated before publication. For help, reporting or account deletion, follow the official support page.
