Mood tracking and journaling app

MoodJot

For people who want a lightweight place to notice and record how a day feels.

Emotions can be difficult to remember in context. MoodJot keeps the current reflection, note, optional image, and reminder close together.

What does it do?

  • Record a mood entry when you want to pause and notice.
  • Add journal notes that preserve the context in your own words.
  • Attach an optional photo and use reminders to return to the practice.

How it works

  1. 1

    Choose the mood that best reflects the moment.

  2. 2

    Add a note or optional photo when context would help.

  3. 3

    Use a reminder as a prompt to check in again.

Data and safety context

Use it deliberately

Entries can contain personal reflections. Decide deliberately what to write or attach and review the product's current privacy information before use.

Important boundary

What is it not?

MoodJot is a journaling aid, not a medical diagnosis or treatment service and does not promise wellbeing outcomes.

Buhane's builder perspective

Why this product?

Buhane built MoodJot to make a short reflective pause easier to capture without turning that moment into a clinical conclusion.

Common questions

Does MoodJot diagnose a mood or condition?

No. It records the information you choose to enter and is not a diagnostic or treatment service.

Do I have to add a photo?

No. Photos are optional; a mood entry or note can stand on its own.

What is the simplest way to begin?

Create one mood entry, add only the context you find useful, and decide whether a reminder fits your routine.
