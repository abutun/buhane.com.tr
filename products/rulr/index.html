RULR

Before you participate

Read the current pricing, terms and refund policy on RULR before any paid challenge. A wallet connection or signature alone does not award a throne.

Important boundary

Fictional game status

A RULR throne does not grant real-world ownership, sovereignty, political authority or financial rights. The product is a game, not an investment or a payout promise.

Buhane's builder perspective

Why this product?

RULR brings public competition to a shared world map. Country status, visible activity and leaderboards give visitors different ways to follow the same game.

Common questions

Do I own a real country?

No. A throne is fictional status within RULR and creates no real-world property or government rights.

How long does a reign last?

The official FAQ says a reign remains visible until a later verified challenge changes the game record.

Where can I review the rules?

The official site publishes its FAQ, pricing, terms, privacy and refund pages. Review them before participating.
