Swipe Slip

For players who enjoy short precision challenges built around movement, timing, and recovery.

Swipe Slip turns a simple navigation goal into a sequence of increasingly demanding tunnel decisions across 200 levels.

Open the official product site

A product built by Buhane Information Technologies. Meet Buhane.

What does it do?

  • Navigate a tunnel by responding to obstacles and openings.
  • Progress through 200 levels whose challenge develops over time.
  • Choose portrait or landscape play to suit the device position.

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick the screen orientation that feels comfortable.

  2. 2

    Swipe to guide movement through the current tunnel challenge.

  3. 3

    Learn from a missed passage and retry the level with adjusted timing.
